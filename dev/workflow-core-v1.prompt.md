# Núcleo 3D da WaveOps

- Referência de movimento e acabamento: https://dg.designboost.com.br/workshop-3ed
- Modo: ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens.
- Arte final: `assets/workflow-core-v1.webp`, com transparência e compressão WebP sem perdas.
- A marca canônica da WaveOps foi aplicada como SVG separado no HTML.
- O painel permanece frontal. Os elementos flutuam em ciclos independentes de 19 a 31 segundos.

## Prompt usado

Create one polished 3D product-render asset for the central automation node of the WaveOps website. Output: a single isolated object on a truly transparent alpha background, square canvas, 512 x 512 preferred, fully within frame with 10% empty margins. Subject: a compact rounded square intelligent processing module, a tangible premium industrial object. Main body satin dark graphite, precision-machined thick beveled frame, soft rounded corners, a luminous deep violet glass front plate with subtle clear edges and beautiful broad studio reflections. The front face is blank with NO letters, NO text, NO logo, NO circuitry drawing, NO symbols; it will receive the site's real vector mark separately. Camera nearly frontal, only 12 degrees of view from above and 12 degrees from the right, enough to reveal a substantial top and right side. Layered violet light seam between glass face and dark body. A few short polished metallic connector pins on left and right edges, four on each side. Rich physical shading, soft violet rim light, subtle warm-white upper-left key light; controlled bloom only right against the object, no big ambient haze. Convincing product photography / high-quality Octane render, sharp crafted object, not a flat app icon, not a cartoon, not an abstract knot. Visual reference direction: realistic isolated 3D tool cubes floating over a near-black premium Brazilian design studio website. No floor, no background rectangle, no shadow outside the object, no other objects, no decorative floating particles. Keep most of front dark purple so a small white brand mark overlaid in code will be highly readable.
